# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 30 July 2026**

Remli is built so that this page can be short.

## What Remli collects

Nothing.

There is no Remli account, no Remli server, and no analytics. The developer cannot see
your ideas, cannot see how you use the app, and has no way to identify you.

## Where your ideas live

On your iPhone.

If you have iCloud enabled, your ideas also sync through **your own** iCloud account so
they are available on your other devices and survive losing your phone. That data belongs
to your Apple ID and is governed by [Apple's privacy policy][apple]. The developer has no
access to it.

## The microphone

When you record an idea, audio is transcribed on your device and then **discarded
immediately**. Remli never stores a recording and never uploads audio anywhere.

## Your calendar

If — and only if — you turn on free-time reminders, Remli reads your calendar to find gaps
in your day. It looks at when you are busy, not at what your events are. Nothing about
your calendar is stored or transmitted.

## Automatic categorisation and connections

By default this runs entirely on your device using Apple's on-device Foundation Models.
Your ideas do not leave your iPhone.

**Optional:** you may supply your own Anthropic API key in Settings to use Claude instead,
which produces better categories and connections. If you do this, the text of an idea is
sent to Anthropic for processing at the moment it is enriched. This is off by default, and
turning it on requires an explicit, separate confirmation that explains exactly what gets
sent. You can turn it off at any time, and doing so stops all outbound requests
immediately. When enabled, Anthropic's handling of that text is governed by their
[privacy policy][anthropic]. Your API key is stored in the iOS Keychain and is never sent
anywhere except to Anthropic.

## Tracking

Remli does not track you. There is no advertising identifier, no third-party SDK, no
cross-app or cross-site tracking, and no data is shared with anyone — including the
developer.

## Children

Remli is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.

## Deleting your data

Delete the app. That removes everything on the device. To remove synced copies, delete
Remli's data from **Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage**.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the date at the top changes with it, and the previous
versions remain visible in this repository's history.
