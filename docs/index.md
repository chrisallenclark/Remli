# Remli

**Capture the idea. Find the thread.**

Remli is an iPhone app for ideas that arrive at bad moments.

Speak or type a thought in a second. Remli titles it, files it into a category drawn from
your own thinking, and links it to your other ideas — telling you *why* they connect.
Then it brings them back to you: at times you choose, when your calendar goes quiet, or in
a weekly review.

## Why it's different

Most notes apps are where ideas go to be forgotten. Remli is built around the opposite
assumption: that the value of an idea shows up later, next to another idea, at a moment
when you have time.

- **Capture never waits.** Your idea is saved the instant you stop talking. Everything
  clever happens afterwards, so nothing is ever lost to a spinner.
- **Connections come with reasons.** Every link says how two ideas relate — *builds on*,
  *unlocks*, *variant of*, *tension with* — and a sentence explaining it.
- **Paths, not just piles.** Because some links are dependencies, Remli can show you the
  route from where you are to a finished idea.
- **Private.** The intelligence runs on your device. Your ideas sync only through your own
  iCloud account. There is no Remli server.
